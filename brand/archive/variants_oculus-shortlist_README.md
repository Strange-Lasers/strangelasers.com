# Strange Lasers Oculus shortlist

This shortlist keeps the mysterious Oculus eye geometry and outlined wordmark identical across eight dark observatory-inspired palettes. Abyss and Moon Crimson are locked references; the other six explore adjacent moods without using green.

## Locked references

- `abyss`: electric cyan and deep cobalt
- `moon-crimson`: moonlit neutral and crimson

## Additional studies

- `blood-moon`: aged bronze and blood red
- `ultraviolet-ash`: spectral violet and cold ash
- `sodium-vapor`: abandoned-lamp amber and bone white
- `deep-field`: ultraviolet and deep-field blue
- `cold-ember`: burnt ember and icy cyan
- `occultation`: ultraviolet and spectral gold

Each horizontal `logo-oculus-*.svg` has a matching square `mark-oculus-*.svg`. Render previews with librsvg or a browser because ImageMagick's internal SVG decoder misrenders the Gaussian glow filters. Run `node generate.mjs` after changing the Oculus source or a palette.
