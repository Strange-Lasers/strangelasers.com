# Strange Lasers Oculus colorways

These color studies use the exact same Oculus eye geometry and outlined wordmark. Only the palette changes.

- `copper-teal`: oxidized copper and teal, the original Oculus palette
- `solar-flare`: hot orange and laser gold
- `ultraviolet-ember`: ultraviolet and ember orange
- `acid-green`: acid lime and emerald
- `abyss`: electric cyan and deep cobalt
- `ice-blue`: cool blue monochrome
- `amber-cobalt`: optical amber and cobalt
- `moon-crimson`: moonlit neutral and crimson

Each horizontal `logo-oculus-*.svg` has a matching square `mark-oculus-*.svg`. The two contact sheets compare the horizontal lockups and square marks. Render previews with librsvg or a browser because ImageMagick's internal SVG decoder misrenders the Gaussian glow filters. Run `node generate.mjs` after changing the Oculus source or a palette.
