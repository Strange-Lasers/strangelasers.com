Strange Lasers

Motion study

One mark, three machines

Each study uses the same depth-aware beam renderer, fixed eye, speed, and master loop. Only the mechanical relationship between the two arcs changes.

01

Rigid rotor

Both arcs share one phase and one rotating axis, preserving Counterflow as a single solid assembly.

02

Quarter-turn gimbal

The axis is shared, while the purple arc remains exactly one quarter-turn ahead of the cyan arc.

03

Synchronized counter-rotation

The axis is shared and the arcs turn at equal speed in opposite directions, meeting on a strict rhythm.

Motion follows your system setting. Add ?animate to override reduced motion.
